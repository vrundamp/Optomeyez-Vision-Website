SMS Communications

You must affirmatively opt in through one of our available consent methods to receive SMS messages from Optomeyez Vision. Consent may be collected through a website form, phone call, in-person conversation, paper form, text message, QR code, or another approved method.

Providing your mobile phone number without affirmatively opting in does not constitute consent.

Messages may include appointment reminders, appointment confirmations, scheduling updates, office notifications, account notifications, and other practice-related communications. Marketing messages will only be sent if you have separately opted in to receive them.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages at any time. Reply HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared, sold, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors that provide support services, such as customer service, is permitted. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared for marketing or promotional purposes or transferred to another business, affiliate, or sender.

For additional information about our SMS program, please review our SMS Terms & Conditions.
